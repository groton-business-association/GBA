Join the GBA

Membership is open to for-profit and non-profit businesses and organizations in Groton and surrounding towns, including storefronts, farms, corporations, home-based businesses, and individual contributors.

Membership types

  • For-profit businessUp to 3 people attend meetings. $200 a year.
  • Non-profit organizationUp to 3 people attend meetings. $200 a year.
  • Non-profit organization, single1 person attends meetings. $100 a year.
  • Individual contributorOnly you attend meetings. $100 a year.

Paying dues

Dues are paid by check. The website does not take payments.

After you send the application, either bring your check to the next GBA meeting or mail it.

Make the check payable to Groton Business Association and mail it to:

Groton Business Association
c/o Jeff Gordon, President

Your listing appears in the member directory once dues are received.
